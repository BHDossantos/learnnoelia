## Full description (App Store & Google Play)

**Noelia is the learning app built around one promise: you don't just finish lessons — you actually remember, understand, and can apply what you learn.**

Most apps reward you for streaks and screen time. Noelia rewards real mastery. Every lesson ends with evidence you've understood it, and skills you mastered weeks ago quietly resurface so they stick for good.

**Learn by doing**
• Hands-on coding labs for Python, SQL, JavaScript, pandas, and data visualization — write real code that runs and is checked instantly, right in the app.
• Interactive quizzes and readings with worked examples across math, science, business, technology, and languages.
• A Socratic coach that asks the right question and guides you to the answer instead of just giving it away.

**Built to make it stick**
• An adaptive engine tracks what you've truly mastered — not just what you clicked through.
• Spaced retention checks bring back older skills at the right moment.
• If you're struggling, Noelia finds the earlier gap and routes you back to fix the root cause.

**Real outcomes**
• Diagnostic placement, a personalized daily plan, and project-based practice.
• Verifiable certificates and Open Badges you can share.
• On-demand human instructors when you want a live session.

Learn a language, pick up coding, prepare for an exam, or master a new field — Noelia adapts to you.

*Subscriptions and plan management are handled on learnnoelia.com. Sign in with your account to access everything you've unlocked.*

- **Does the app collect/share user data?** Yes (collect). **Sell data?** No.
  **Use data for ads / third-party advertising?** No.
